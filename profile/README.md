# Welcome to Food, gut & nutritional metabolomics research group

We are a research team based in Turku and Kuopio, Finland. We use
metabolomics to investigate different scientific topics in food
science and nutrition.

# Research

The key interests of our group include:

- The biochemical profiles of foods and how they are altered
  by food processing
- How diet can beneficially affect health
- How gut microbiota is linked with the diet in affecting
  overall and brain health

To know more about us, visit
[research](https://hanhinevalab.utu.fi/index.php/research/) and
[publications](https://hanhinevalab.utu.fi/index.php/144-2/) pages on our
website. Also, check out our outreach material on
[GitHub](https://github.com/hanhineva-lab/outreach).

# Team

Our team is led by professor Kati Hanhineva and is based at the University of
Turku, Department of Life Technologies and the University of Eastern Finland,
Institute of Public Health and Clinical Nutrition.

To know more about team members and their projects, visit our
